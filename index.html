From Cirebon to the world

Rotan Ridwan is a rattan handicraft export business owned by Mr. Ridwan in Cirebon, a city long known as Indonesia's rattan hub. Every product is woven by local artisans from selected rattan that is dried and varnished, then packed securely for international shipping.

  • HandwovenTraditional weaving techniques, with each product made one by one.
  • Secure packagingWrapped in layers to arrive intact by sea or by air.
  • Six destination countriesSingapore, Malaysia, Japan, Australia, the Netherlands, and the United States.
